Features

Everything Finder does, for iCloud.

🗂️

Finder-native

Icons, list and columns, a preview pane, tabs and two panes side by side. Quick Look, Get Info and your usual shortcuts all work.

🖐️

Drag anything anywhere

Move files between iCloud Drive, Photos, your disks, cameras and Finder. Every move goes through a transfer queue with progress, retries and size checks.

🌄

Photos, properly

See your library by month, with albums, Live Photos, favorites and Recently Deleted. Exports keep the originals and their capture dates.

🔎

Search, tags, rename

Search through subfolders, add Finder tags and rename many files at once. Also: folder sizes, New File, Make Alias and Open in Terminal.

💾

Back up & sync

Back up all of iCloud to any drive as ordinary folders, with photos sorted by year and month. Sync jobs copy, mirror or two-way sync, and you always see a preview first.

⇄

iCloud ⇄ Google

Move to Google Drive and Google Photos, or back again (Google Takeout included). Your albums come along, and nothing is ever deleted.

🔑

Any Apple ID

Use your device's own iCloud, or sign in to any Apple ID the way iCloud.com does, with two-factor and Advanced Data Protection.

🛡️

Safe by design

Removals only ever go to the Trash or Recently Deleted. In read-only mode, nothing in iCloud can change at all.

🔒

Private

Cirrus talks only to Apple and Google, with no analytics and no servers of its own. Passwords stay on your device behind Touch ID or Face ID.

iPhone & iPad

The iCloud app your iPhone should have had.

The same engine in a SwiftUI app: open a second Apple ID, export full-quality originals into Files, or back up all of iCloud to a USB drive.

  • Several Apple IDs, with Face ID for saved passwords
  • Exports with Live Photo videos and RAW files, sorted by year and month
  • Back up to a USB-C drive, SD card or file server
  • A transfer queue that keeps going in the background

Questions

Is Cirrus made by Apple?

No. It's an independent open-source project. It uses the same web services as iCloud.com, or your device's own iCloud.

Does it need my Apple ID password?

Only to add an Apple ID. It goes only to Apple, using the same secure sign-in as iCloud.com. Cirrus keeps it only if you ask, on this device and behind Touch ID or Face ID.

Can it delete my stuff by accident?

Removals only go to the Trash or Recently Deleted. Sync runs show a preview first and stop if they would remove an unusual number of files. Read-only mode blocks every change.

macOS says it can't open the app?

Releases aren't notarized yet. Open it once from System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway, or build it yourself.
